Predictions blend a possession model with DraftKings lines and ESPN BPI. Player numbers are projections from the last 10 games, not guarantees. Injury statuses come from the latest official NBA report when the data was built; teams that had not yet submitted use ESPN. Check again close to tip-off.

How these predictions are made
  • Possession model: game pace is the average of both teams' pace. Each team scores pace/100 × (its offensive rating + the opponent's defensive rating)/2, plus 1 point at home and minus 1 away.
  • The predicted margin and total are 50% possession model and 50% DraftKings line.
  • Win % is the average of three numbers: the blended margin turned into a probability (spread of 12 points), the vig-free moneyline, and ESPN BPI.
  • Confidence is High when win % is at least 75% and all three agree on the winner, Medium from 60%, otherwise Low. Blowout concern is Elevated at a 10+ point margin and Moderate at 6+.
  • Players: per-minute rate = 60% last 10, 25% season, 15% last 5, times expected minutes, adjusted at half strength for the opponent's defence and pace. Minutes left by teammates now Out or Doubtful are shared out. The range is ±0.8 standard deviations of the last 10 games.
